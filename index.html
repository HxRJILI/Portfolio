I'm an AI & Data Technologies engineer finishing my degree at ENSAM Meknès, and across three roles at OCP Group and LeanFlow Professionals I've kept coming back to the same question: how does a model become a decision someone downstream can actually trust and act on?

"Build it, ship it, prove its value — a demand-forecasting engine for a national industrial producer, an LLM-based sourcing pipeline that replaced hours of manual research, and a diagnostics tool that turned scattered plant data into a single fixable narrative."

My work sits at the intersection of technical depth and business framing: deep learning and reinforcement learning for forecasting and scheduling, retrieval-augmented generation and LLM agents for market intelligence, and computer vision for real-time operational systems — always translated back into what a planner, an analyst, or a plant manager can execute. That translation layer is where I've spent the most time, and where I want to keep building: as a consultant who can go from a stakeholder's problem statement to a working, defensible AI system and back again.

Beyond delivery, I've led the AI Cell and served as Vice-President at Gadz'IT ENSAM, co-organized an AI literacy bootcamp for 50+ students, and contributed an oblique-classifier implementation to the open-source ML ecosystem. Structuring a technical roadmap into something a room of non-technical stakeholders can act on is, to me, the same skill as structuring a client recommendation.
